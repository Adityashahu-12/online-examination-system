Core Capabilities

Engineered for Academic Rigor and Operational Integrity

A unified platform integrating conflict-free scheduling, timed online testing, and automated evaluation.

📝

Online Examination

Full-screen interactive test interface featuring server-synchronized countdown timers, question palette navigation, real-time autosave, and mark-for-review status tracking.

📅

Examination Scheduling

Mathematical conflict prevention powered by PostgreSQL btree_gist exclusion constraints, ensuring zero overlapping shifts in the same lab or center.

⚡

Automated Evaluation

Instant grading engine computing correct scores, negative markings, percentage calculations, and pass/fail verdicts immediately upon submission.

📊

Result Management

Detailed institutional scorecards, grade transcripts, performance analytics, and administrative publication controls with complete auditability.

🔒

Secure Authentication

Robust bcrypt password hashing, session-based role authorization (Admin and Student), parameter sanitization, and brute-force protection.

🏛️

Administrative Management

Complete institutional control over departments, courses, exam centers, master examination authoring, question banks, and student assignments.

About ExamHub

Designed for Enterprise Academic Administration

ExamHub was developed to address critical scheduling challenges in higher education, eliminating room booking clashes and providing an integrated assessment lifecycle from question authoring to score publication.

  • ✓ Built with Node.js, Express, and PostgreSQL 16
  • ✓ Range exclusion constraints for conflict-free room scheduling
  • ✓ Server-side countdown enforcement preventing client-side timer tampering
  • ✓ Fully responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop environments
